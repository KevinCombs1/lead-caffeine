XBRL-Verified (highest confidence) — Reads actual SEC 10-K filings via 76+ XBRL taxonomy concepts. If a company files with the SEC, we can see their R&D expenses, foreign tax credits, deferred tax liabilities, PP&E values, employee counts, etc. directly from their balance sheet. This is the gold standard.

Inferred (medium confidence) — Cross-references intelligence from 25+ government data sources (USPTO patents, EPA facilities, OSHA violations, DOE grants, SBIR awards, etc.) with SIC industry codes to infer eligibility. For example: if a company has SBIR awards + patents, they almost certainly qualify for R&D credits.

Estimated by Industry (lowest confidence) — Uses SIC code and industry category matching. If you're in manufacturing (SIC 2000-3999), you're matched to applicable credits for that industry even without specific evidence.

How Dollar Amounts Are Estimated
There are two estimation methods:

XBRL-based: When we have actual financial data, we calculate directly. For example, if a company reports $10M in R&D expense but shows no R&D credit activity, we estimate 6.5% of that = $650K unclaimed.

Heuristic industry medians: When we lack specific financial data, each credit has a per-company median value based on typical mid-size company claims. These get scaled by a "match strength" multiplier (how many signals confirm eligibility).

1. R&D Tax Credit (IRC §41) — Low neglect
What: 6-8% of qualified research expenses
Detection: XBRL-verified via researchCredit, researchCarryforward, researchRateImpact, and rdExpense fields. If R&D expense exists but no credit activity shows, flagged as unclaimed.
$ Estimate: 6.5% of reported R&D expense. Heuristic median: $125K.
Why it matters: The most widely applicable credit. Every company doing product development, software, or process improvement likely qualifies, but many still don't claim it properly.
2. Work Opportunity Tax Credit / WOTC (IRC §51) — High neglect
What: $2,400–$9,600 per qualifying hire (veterans, SNAP recipients, ex-felons, etc.)
Detection: Employee count from XBRL + general credits check. If a company has 50+ employees and no employment credits, flagged. Assumes 5% qualifying hire rate.
$ Estimate: min(employees × 5%, 100) × $4,800. Heuristic median: $24K.
Why it matters: High neglect because the paperwork must be filed within 28 days of hire. Most companies don't know to do this.
3. Employee Retention Credit / ERC (IRC §3134) — High neglect, SUNSET

$ Estimate: Heuristic median: $50K.
Why it matters: Retroactive only via amended returns. The IRS is still processing legitimate claims. Many qualifying companies never filed.
