The Batch Strategy
Batch 1 — "The Foundation" (6 → 15 sources)
The highest-value no-auth sources that immediately thicken every tab:

Treasury Fiscal Data (debt, spending, revenue, exchange rates, bond auctions)
FDIC BankFind (every bank, quarterly financials, failures)
OpenFEMA (disaster declarations, assistance grants, flood insurance)
BLS v1 (unemployment, CPI, wages, JOLTS — no registration)
openFDA (drug/device approvals, recalls, adverse events)
Census Bureau (county business patterns, international trade)
NHTSA (vehicle recalls, safety data)
World Bank (global indicators)
ExchangeRate-API (currency rates)
This batch covers all 4 pillars and all 11 tabs get real data. We also refactor the connector framework to be config-driven here, so every batch after this is faster.

Batch 2 — "Deepen the Currents" (15 → 25 sources)
Sub-endpoints within existing sources + new ones:

SEC EDGAR expanded (Form 4 insider trading, 13F institutional holdings, proxy statements)
Treasury expanded (daily statements, TIPS, savings bonds)
OpenFEMA expanded (public assistance details, hazard mitigation)
USGS (earthquakes, water resources)
Open-Meteo (weather/climate for agriculture and energy correlation)
CoinGecko (crypto market data)
CDC WONDER (health surveillance)
USDA FAS (agricultural trade)
Batch 3 — "The Intelligence Layer" (25 → 50+ sources)
Census sub-endpoints (50+ datasets), Treasury sub-endpoints (20+), openFDA sub-endpoints (15), plus building the convergence detection algorithm. This is where the system stops just collecting and starts thinking.

Batch 4+ — "The Long Tail" (50 → 330)
Config-driven at this point. Each new stream is just a config entry — URL, field mappings, category, current. Mass onboarding. The architecture does the heavy lifting.

The Architecture Shift
The key insight: Batch 1 isn't just adding 9 sources. It's rebuilding the connector framework so that Batch 2 takes half the time, Batch 3 takes a quarter, and Batch 4 is mostly configuration.

That means Batch 1 includes:

A generic ConfigurableConnector that reads from a source config object
A background scheduler with staggered fetch intervals
Rate limit management across all sources
A unified normalization pipeline
After Batch 1, adding a new source is a config object instead of a new function.

When to Build the Convergence Algorithm
After Batch 1. We need enough data variety across all three currents (transactions, trends, timelines) before the algorithm has anything meaningful to detect. 9 more sources gives us that critical mass.
